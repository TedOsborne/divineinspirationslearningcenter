Concrete Patio Contractor in Biloxi, MS

Professional Concrete Solutions plans and builds concrete patios around the property, the people using it, and the way rainwater moves through the yard.

Professional Concrete Solutions is a concrete patio contractor in Biloxi, MS, creating practical outdoor surfaces for meals, gatherings, grilling, quiet mornings, and everyday family time. The best Gulf Coast patio plans also account for drainage, access, furniture placement, and connections to the house and yard.

At a glance
  • Plan the furniture, grill, shade, and walking routes before setting dimensions.
  • Choose the elevation and slope with Gulf Coast rain in mind.
  • Select a finish that balances appearance, traction, comfort, and upkeep.
  • Consider future walkways, steps, slabs, or patio extensions now.

Start with how you want to use the patio

Square footage alone does not make a patio comfortable. A useful plan begins with real activities. Will the space hold a dining table, lounge seating, a grill, planters, a fire feature, or a play area? Does someone need a clear route from the back door to the yard? Will the patio support frequent gatherings or mostly serve two people?

Place temporary markers in the yard and arrange the furniture footprint before deciding on the final dimensions. Leave room to move chairs, open doors, walk around a table, and keep hot equipment separated from normal traffic. This small planning step helps prevent a finished patio that feels crowded or leaves unusable corners.

Design around daily life

A patio should solve a space problem, not just fill a rectangle.

The shape can respond to doors, rooflines, landscaping, views, shade, and walking routes. A simple layout often looks more finished when every edge has a purpose.

Evaluate the site and drainage before choosing a finish

South Mississippi properties can receive intense rain, so water movement belongs near the top of the planning list. The patio should be coordinated with the home's foundation, door thresholds, yard grade, downspouts, existing drains, and any low areas. Water should not be trapped against the home or sent toward a place where it creates erosion or standing water.

The contractor should also review access for preparation and concrete placement, the condition of the soil, roots or old material in the work area, and how the new patio will meet grass, landscaping, pools, walkways, or existing concrete. Tight access can affect equipment, labor, and scheduling, even when the patio itself is modest.

Choose a patio size and layout that fits the property

Dining and entertaining zones

Allow space for the table or seating group plus comfortable circulation around it. If the grill or outdoor kitchen will be used often, think about serving paths and where smoke or heat may travel. Separate zones can be created through shape, scoring, furniture placement, or a subtle change in orientation.

Transitions from the house

The relationship between the back door, steps, and patio surface affects both appearance and convenience. A well-planned transition feels natural, limits awkward level changes, and gives people a clear route between indoor and outdoor spaces.

Room to grow

If a future pergola, screened enclosure, walkway, shed slab, pool connection, or patio extension is likely, mention it during the first consultation. Even when those features are not part of the current project, knowing the longer-term plan can help avoid conflicts.

Concrete patio finish options

Broom finish

A clean, practical texture that works well for many outdoor living spaces and is straightforward to maintain.

Decorative scoring

Planned lines can create visual rhythm, define zones, and give a simple slab a more tailored appearance.

Borders and accents

A contrasting edge or carefully chosen detail can connect the patio to the architecture without overwhelming it.

Coordinated walkways

Matching or complementary paths can make the patio feel connected to gates, driveways, gardens, and other destinations.

Before selecting a highly decorative surface, ask how it will feel underfoot, how it handles wet conditions, what maintenance it may need, and whether the look fits the home. Samples and project photos can help, but the final choice should also work for the actual site.

Outdoor concrete features that can complete the space

A patio often works better as part of a small network of surfaces. Concrete walkways can provide a stable path to a driveway, gate, garden, pool, or detached structure. Steps can resolve elevation changes. A slab can support a shed, equipment area, or other approved use. Extensions can add a grilling zone or room for a larger seating arrangement.

Planning related work together may create cleaner transitions and a more consistent finish. It also gives the contractor a fuller view of access, drainage, and how each part of the property needs to function.

What to share when requesting a patio quote

Provide the property address, approximate dimensions, a few wide photos, and pictures of the route from the street or driveway to the backyard. Explain how you plan to use the space and identify doors, low spots, downspouts, utilities, trees, fences, and existing surfaces. If you have a preferred shape or finish, include example photos.

Common Questions

Clear answers for your next concrete project.

What is a practical finish for a concrete patio?

A broom finish is a popular practical choice for outdoor concrete. Decorative scoring, borders, and other details can be considered based on appearance, traction, maintenance, and budget.

How should a patio handle rainwater?

The elevation and slope should be coordinated with the house, yard, downspouts, drains, and nearby surfaces so water does not collect against the home or in avoidable low spots.

Can a concrete patio be extended later?

Many patios can be extended, but planning future additions early can produce cleaner transitions and reduce conflicts with drainage, landscaping, utilities, and other improvements.

Do you build walkways and steps with patios?

Yes. Professional Concrete Solutions provides patios, walkways, sidewalks, steps, slabs, and related flatwork for Biloxi and Mississippi Gulf Coast properties.
