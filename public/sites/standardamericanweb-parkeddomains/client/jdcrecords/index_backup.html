"Just Don't Change"

JDC Records (Just Don't Change Records) is the hottest record production company in the South. Featuring the latest hip hop artists in the industry. We are an innovative brand associated with the marketing of music recordings and music videos. JDC RECORDS also provide publicists, who assist our artists in gaining positive media coverage and arrange for their merchandise to be available via stores and other media outlets. The CEO is the notorious Palaya Pouncho who has an extensive background in music production that spans over 15 years.
