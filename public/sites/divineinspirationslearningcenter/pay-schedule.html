Welcome to the Pay Schedule page of Divine Inspirations Learning Center. It is essential that all parents and guardians understand our payment policies to ensure the smooth operation of our center. Timely payments are crucial for maintaining the high quality of care and education we provide. Please review the following information carefully to avoid any misunderstandings regarding payment expectations and deadlines.

"To avoid any disruption in your child's care, please ensure all payments are made on time."- Jackie Conerly

Payments are due on the first day of each month and can be made through our secure online payment system or in person at our office. Late payments will incur a late fee of $25, which will be added to your account if payment is not received within five days of the due date. Consistent late payments may result in the suspension of your child’s enrollment until the account is settled. We appreciate your prompt attention to this matter to ensure uninterrupted care for your child.

We understand that unexpected circumstances can arise. If you anticipate any difficulty in making a payment on time, please contact our office as soon as possible to discuss alternative arrangements. Our priority is to work with you to ensure your child's continued care and education. Clear communication and prompt action can help us address any issues and maintain a positive relationship. Thank you for your cooperation and commitment to our payment policies.
