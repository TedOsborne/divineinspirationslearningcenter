Meet The Owner

Meet Jackie Conerly, the heart and soul behind Divine Inspirations Learning Center in Gautier, MS. With over 20 years of experience in childcare, Jackie is passionate about providing a safe, nurturing, and educational environment for all children. Her dedication to fostering emotional, social, and cognitive development through Christian values sets her apart. Discover how her commitment to excellence and love for teaching can make a difference in your child’s life.

  • Extensive Experience: 20 years of childcare experience.
  • Jackie integrates Christian teachings into daily activities.
  • Focuses on the complete growth of each child.
  • Personalized Care: Understanding that every child is unique needs.
How do you ensure the safety of the children in your care?

Safety is our top priority at Divine Inspirations Learning Center. Our facility is equipped with secure entry systems, and all staff members are trained in first aid and CPR. We also conduct regular safety drills and maintain strict supervision policies to ensure a safe environment for all children.

What kind of curriculum do you use?

Our curriculum is designed to foster holistic development in children, focusing on emotional, social, cognitive, and physical growth. We integrate Christian teachings to instill strong moral values and provide a well-rounded educational experience.

How do you handle discipline and behavioral issues?

We believe in positive reinforcement and use gentle, respectful approaches to discipline. Our goal is to guide children towards understanding acceptable behavior and developing self-control. We work closely with parents to address any behavioral concerns.
